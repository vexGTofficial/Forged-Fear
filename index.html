FORGED FEAR // LEGAL

PRIVACY
POLICY

LAST UPDATED // AUGUST 23, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Forged Fear handles player data.

Forged Fear uses Photon Voice for voice chat, Photon Cloud for multiplayer networking, and PlayFab for cosmetics, accounts, and moderation.
01 // INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Player Data

Forged Fear uses several third-party services to provide multiplayer and gameplay functionality.

Photon Voice

Processes live voice audio for real-time communication.

Audio is not stored by Forged Fear.

Photon Multiplayer

Handles multiplayer networking, routing and session data.

Forged Fear does not store this networking data ourselves.

PlayFab

Stores player IDs, cosmetics, ban status, device information and region.

This information is used for gameplay features and moderation.

02 // HOW WE USE DATA

Why Data Is Used

We use data only for multiplayer functionality, voice chat, cosmetics, moderation and improving game performance.

We do not sell player data.

03 // THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

External Providers

Photon Voice, Photon Cloud and PlayFab are third-party services used by Forged Fear.

These services have their own privacy policies and terms that govern how information processed by their platforms is handled.

04 // DATA STORAGE

Where Data Is Stored

Forged Fear does not store personal data on its own servers.

Persistent player data used by the game is stored through PlayFab.

05 // IN-GAME PURCHASES

Purchases

Forged Fear includes optional in-game purchases.

Purchases may involve information being processed by the platform or payment provider through which the purchase is made.

Forged Fear does not directly store complete payment card information.

Purchase-related information may also be used to provide purchased in-game content and prevent unauthorized use.

06 // CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Age Requirement

Forged Fear is not intended for children under the age of 13.

07 // CONTACT

Questions?

For privacy-related questions or concerns, contact LoopHole Studios through the official Forged Fear Discord server.
